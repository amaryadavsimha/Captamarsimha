Indian Army · Officer entry · Reference

Routes to a Commission

Compiled 14 Sep 2026
Course numbers and vacancies move every cycle. Read the live notification before you act on anything here.

There is no single door into the officer cadre. There are sixteen, and which ones are open to you is decided almost entirely by one thing: the highest qualification you already hold, and whether you are already in uniform. That is how this page is arranged — not alphabetically, and not by glamour, but by the gate each route opens from.

Straight from Class 12

4 routes · ages 16½–25

The school-leaver routes are the cheapest commissions in the service — the Army pays for the degree — and the most competitive. All four are age-gated hard; miss the window and it never reopens.

National Defence Academy · NDA & NA

Permanent Commission

The classic route. A UPSC examination twice a year, three years at Khadakwasla alongside cadets bound for the Navy and Air Force, then a year at the IMA.

Age
16.5 – 19.5 years
Who
Unmarried men and women
Qualification
Class 12 pass — any stream for the Army wing; Physics, Chemistry and Maths for the Navy and Air Force wings
Selection
UPSC written (Mathematics + General Ability) → 5-day SSB → medical → all-India merit
Training
3 years NDA Khadakwasla + 1 year IMA Dehradun
Cycle
Twice a year — NDA I and NDA II

Technical Entry Scheme · 10+2 TES

Permanent Commission

No Army written paper at all. Your JEE Main score does the sifting, and the Army then puts you through an engineering degree at its own colleges before you command anything.

Age
16.5 – 19.5 years
Who
Unmarried men
Qualification
Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry and Maths, minimum 60% aggregate across the three, plus a valid JEE Main score
Selection
Shortlisting on JEE Main → SSB → medical
Training
5 years — 1 year basic military training, 3 years engineering at CME Pune, MCTE Mhow or MCEME Secunderabad, 1 year post-commission
Scale
TES-56 carried roughly 90 vacancies
Worth knowing You are commissioned before the degree is finished, and you draw a Lieutenant's pay through the final year of study. The trade is a longer bond and a technical arm posting.

Military Nursing Service · B.Sc Nursing

Commission in the MNS

A four-year nursing degree at an Armed Forces college, ending in a commission as a Lieutenant. Run entirely by the Armed Forces Medical Services, on its own calendar and its own portal.

Age
17 – 25 years
Who
Women only — unmarried, or divorced, legally separated or widowed without dependants
Qualification
Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English, minimum 50% aggregate; NEET-UG score required
Selection
NEET-UG → MNS test of general intelligence and English → psychological assessment → interview → medical
Apply at
join.afms.gov.in — not the Army recruiting portal

Armed Forces Medical College · AFMC Pune

Bonded commission in the AFMS

An MBBS at Pune, followed by a bonded commission in the medical services. Selection runs off NEET-UG plus AFMC's own screening, and the year's rules are set by the college rather than by Army recruiting.

Qualification
Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English; NEET-UG
Who
Men and women, per the year's prospectus
Obligation
Compulsory service bond on graduation
Apply at
join.afms.gov.in — check the current prospectus for age, marital status and the bond terms

Any degree, any discipline

4 routes · ages 18–42

What your degree is in stops mattering here; what matters is whether you sit a written paper. Two of these four routes skip it entirely.

CDS → Indian Military Academy

Permanent Commission

The graduate equivalent of the NDA, and the only graduate route to a permanent commission in the combat arms on day one.

Age
19 – 24 years
Who
Unmarried men
Qualification
A degree from a recognised university, any discipline
Selection
UPSC CDS written (English, General Knowledge, Elementary Mathematics) → SSB → medical → merit
Training
IMA Dehradun, 18 months
Cycle
Twice a year — CDS I and CDS II

CDS → Officers Training Academy · SSC Non-Technical

Short Service Commission

The widest graduate door in the Army, and the one open to both men and women. The Elementary Mathematics paper is dropped for the OTA course.

Age
19 – 25 years
Who
Unmarried men and women; separate notified categories exist for widows of defence personnel
Qualification
A degree from a recognised university, any discipline
Selection
UPSC CDS written (English, General Knowledge) → SSB → medical → merit
Tenure
10 years, extendable to 14; Permanent Commission may be applied for
Training
OTA Chennai, 49 weeks

NCC Special Entry

Short Service Commission

No written examination whatsoever. Three years in the NCC Senior Division buys you a direct call to the Service Selection Board — which is why the vacancy count is small and the competition is fierce.

Age
19 – 25 years
Who
Unmarried men and women, notified separately
Qualification
A degree with minimum 50% aggregate, plus an NCC 'C' certificate graded B or above and two to three years in the Senior Division or Senior Wing
Selection
Direct SSB on NCC credentials → medical → merit
Tenure
10 years, extendable to 14; Permanent Commission may be applied for after 10
Training
OTA Chennai, 49 weeks
Ring-fenced vacancies A recent men's course carried 70 vacancies, of which 7 were reserved for wards of battle casualties. Those candidates are exempted from the NCC 'C' certificate requirement and certify instead through the Manpower (Policy & Planning) Directorate.

Territorial Army

Part-time commission

The only route on this page that does not ask you to leave your job. It also carries the widest age band in the officer cadre by a margin of fifteen years.

Age
18 – 42 years
Who
Men and women in gainful civil employment or self-employment. Serving regular and paramilitary personnel are not eligible
Qualification
A degree from a recognised university
Selection
Computer-based test — 100 questions in 2 hours (Reasoning 40, General Knowledge 30, English 30), with one-third negative marking → interview board → SSB → medical
Commitment
About two months' training a year, with liability for embodiment on operational requirement

A professional degree

5 routes · ages 20–45

Engineering, law, medicine and dentistry each have a dedicated channel. None of them involves a written examination — your degree marks, your CLAT PG score or your NEET score do the shortlisting.

Technical Graduate Course · TGC

Permanent Commission

The engineer's permanent commission. Shortlisting runs off your engineering aggregate alone, which makes the cut-off percentage the whole contest.

Age
20 – 27 years
Who
Unmarried men
Qualification
BE or B.Tech in a notified stream
Selection
Shortlisting on engineering aggregate → SSB → medical → merit
Training
IMA Dehradun, 1 year

Short Service Commission (Technical) · SSC Tech

Short Service Commission

The largest technical intake, notified separately for men and women, and the one that publishes vacancies stream by stream — so you can read your own odds before applying.

Age
20 – 27 years
Who
Unmarried men and women, notified separately
Qualification
BE or B.Tech in a notified stream; final-year students may apply against a completion deadline
Selection
Direct SSB on engineering marks → medical → merit
Tenure
10 years, extendable to 14; Permanent Commission may be applied for
Training
OTA Gaya or OTA Chennai, 49 weeks
A recent cycle, by stream 350 vacancies: Mechanical 101, Civil 75, Electronics & Communication 64, Computer Science & IT 60, Electrical 33, Ballistics & Laser Technology 17.

Judge Advocate General · JAG

Short Service Commission

The narrowest gate in the service. Vacancies run in single digits per course, and a CLAT PG score is now mandatory — which quietly turned an application into a national examination.

Age
21 – 27 years
Who
Unmarried men and women, notified separately
Qualification
LLB with minimum 55% aggregate from a Bar Council–recognised institution, eligible for enrolment as an advocate, plus a valid CLAT PG score
Selection
Shortlisting on CLAT PG → SSB → medical → merit
Tenure
10 years, extendable to 14; Permanent Commission may be applied for after 10
Training
OTA Chennai, 49 weeks
Scale The 123rd course carried 5 vacancies for men. Treat JAG as a route you attempt alongside another, not instead of one.

Army Medical Corps · AMC

Short Service Commission

The only officer entries that skip the Service Selection Board entirely. An AFMS board interviews you instead, and the process is measured in weeks rather than months.

Age
Up to 30 years, with relaxation per government norms
Who
Men and women
Qualification
MBBS recognised by the National Medical Commission, internship complete
Selection
Shortlisting → interview before an AFMS board → medical
Scale
A recent cycle carried about 100 vacancies, roughly 75 men and 25 women
Apply at
join.afms.gov.in

Army Dental Corps · AD Corps

Short Service Commission

The most forgiving age limit of any direct entry — a practising dentist in their forties is still eligible, which is true of nothing else on this page.

Age
Up to 45 years, no relaxation
Who
Men and women
Qualification
BDS with minimum 55% in the final examination, or MDS, from a Dental Council–recognised institution, with permanent registration; a valid NEET-MDS score is the shortlisting instrument
Selection
Shortlisting on NEET-MDS → interview at an Army base hospital → medical
Apply at
join.afms.gov.in

From the ranks

3 routes · serving soldiers only

Three departmental routes, separated by how much service you already have behind you. These are the only entries that admit married candidates as a matter of course, and the only ones where your record of service is itself part of the application.

Army Cadet College · ACC

Permanent Commission

The full commission, with no ceiling on where it ends. Four years of training — longer than any other route bar TES — and the graduate emerges indistinguishable from a direct-entry officer.

Age
20 – 27 years
Service
Minimum 2 years
Qualification
Class 12 or equivalent, plus the ACC written examination
Selection
ACC written → SSB → medical → merit
Training
3 years at the ACC Wing, Dehradun + 1 year IMA
Marital status
Married candidates eligible

Special Commissioned Officer · SCO

Commission with a rank ceiling

For the soldier who has already built a career and wants the commission that follows from it. The rank ceiling is lower than a direct entry's — that is the trade, and it is explicit in the terms.

Age
Roughly 30 – 35 years; the opening age varies by cycle
Service
Minimum 5 years
Qualification
Matriculation with a one-year diploma, or higher
Selection
Written examination → SSB → medical
Training
Pre-commission capsule, then 1 year at IMA Dehradun
Marital status
Married candidates eligible

Permanent Commission (Special List) · PC(SL)

Permanent Commission

The long-service route. Twelve weeks of training against four years at the ACC, because the Army is commissioning experience it has already spent a decade building.

Age
Up to 42 years — 45 for Inspector, Army Band
Service
Minimum 10 years as a JCO or NCO
Qualification
Class 11 or 10+2, with additional requirements by category; SHAPE-1 medical category
Selection
Departmental screening → SSB, maximum 3 attempts → medical
Training
12 weeks — an 8-week common skill capsule plus 4 weeks physical training
Marital status
Married candidates eligible

True of every route

Whatever gate you come through, four things do not change.

Nationality

Indian citizen. Subjects of Nepal, and persons of Indian origin migrated from specified countries, are eligible for the UPSC entries subject to a certificate of eligibility.

The Service Selection Board

Five days, two stages. Day one screens with the Officer Intelligence Rating and Picture Perception & Discussion Test; those who clear it go on to the psychologist, the Group Testing Officer and the interviewing officer, all assessing the same fifteen Officer-Like Qualities. The medical entries are the exception — they interview instead.

Medical

A Special Medical Board examines every recommended candidate. Appeal and review boards exist and are worth using; a temporary rejection is frequently recoverable.

Marital status

Nearly every direct entry requires you to be unmarried at commissioning, and to stay unmarried through training. The departmental routes and the Territorial Army are the exceptions.

Four things people still get wrong

UES is closed
The University Entry Scheme, which commissioned pre-final-year engineering students through campus selection, is no longer run. TGC and SSC (Tech) cover the same ground.
Agnipath is not an officer scheme
Agnipath changed enrolment into the ranks; it did not touch officer entry. None of the sixteen routes above recruit Agniveers as officers. An Agniveer who is retained and later qualifies competes for ACC, SCO and PC(SL) on the same terms as any other soldier.
Two portals, not one
MNS, AMC, the Dental Corps and AFMC run on join.afms.gov.in, on the medical services' own calendar. Everything else is notified on joinindianarmy.nic.in. Watching only one of the two is how people miss a window.
A note on corps
Corps-specific inductions — the Army Education Corps among them — appear as notified categories inside the graduate and technical entries above rather than as standing schemes of their own. Read the category list in each live notification.
