Before you apply

Four things to remember

The route changes. These basics show up again and again.

Nationality

Indian citizens are eligible. Some Nepalese subjects and specified persons of Indian origin may also qualify under the notified rules.

SSB

Most routes use the five-day, two-stage SSB process. It looks at officer potential through psychology, group tasks and interview.

Medical

Every recommended candidate faces a medical board. Appeal and review provisions exist where applicable.

Marital status

Many direct entries require candidates to be unmarried during training. Territorial Army and departmental routes are different.

Two portals, not one.
For MNS, AMC, Dental Corps and AFMC, check join.afms.gov.in. Most other Army officer entries are notified through joinindianarmy.nic.in. Always read the current notification.
Common confusion: UES is no longer a current entry scheme. Agnipath is an enrolment route into the ranks, not an officer-entry scheme. Serving personnel who later qualify use the applicable departmental routes.
